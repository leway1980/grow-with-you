# M3 implementation plan: junior high, senior high, leaving home, plus engine (陪你長大)

Canon is docs/DESIGN.md. Numbers marked "design estimate" are for the planner or teacher to confirm.

## 0. What the code assumes today (things that break)

- **`data/calendar.json`**
  - `totalTurns:92`, `playableTurns:56`.
  - Preschool: 12 turns at 4-month steps.
  - School years: 6 segments per year, found by **index** (`schoolFixed.seg:0..4`).
  - `fixed` uses absolute turns 1, 10 and 20.
  - `flavor` has no lines for 國中, 高中 or 離家.
- **`js/engine/turn.js`**
  - `buildTimeline` computes `ageMonths = 72+(g-1)*12+k*2`, so it assumes 6 segments.
  - `startTurn`/`finishTurn` end the game at `playableTurns` with reason "待續".
  - `drift` has a `base3c` table by stage.
- **`js/engine/state.js`**: `START_TURN={birth:0, elementary:20}`; `SAVE_VERSION=1`.
- **`js/engine/family.js:230`**: `monthsAtTurn` hard-codes the 92-turn layout. `identities.json siblings.youngerTurn:[10,40]` is turn-based. `familyCtx(f, role, st.turn)` uses the turn for siblings.
- **Turn-based windows** — these change meaning when density changes:
  - `tags.json repetition.window:6`
  - `ending.json records[].window:6`
  - `routePosition` uses the last 12 curve points
  - `avgVis` n=8/12
  - `parentModel.emaTurns:6`
  - `followUp.after:[a,b]`, `opens.turns`, `cooldown`
  - mech "3C 6 回合內 ≥3"
  - `simulate.mjs` `money30 = vcurves[30]` and `e = birth?20:0`
- **Approach exit streak by stage** (`tags.json approach.exitStreak`: 2/2/3/4/5/6). There is no entry for 離家, and 高中=6 is impossible with 6 turns.
- **Hard-coded stage lists**: `tools/metrics.mjs PLAYED_STAGES`, `tools/lint-content.mjs:76 stageNames`, and `tools/obviousness.mjs` (start-mode heuristic).
- **Hard-coded numbers in tests**: 56 in `engine`, `save`, `coherence` and `growth`; 20, 55 and 36 in `turn`, `tags` and `persist`. `parent_self.test` requires ≥6 cards per stage, which makes junior and senior high mandatory.
- **Cards without `stages`** load into every stage (`prepareData`). Today only the 3 `_fixed` cards lack it, but lint must start requiring it.
- **`events.js buildCtx`** whitelists `st.vars` explicitly, so new route and habit variables must be registered there.
- **`ending.js`**: `achievement.note` is hard-coded to "國小畢業了…先寫到這裡". `keyMoments`/`estimateDeltas` ignore plasticity.
- **DESIGN inconsistencies to fix in E2**:
  - §1 line 20 says the start-from-grade-1 mode has 54 decisions; with 72 turns it is 72−24 = **48 turns**, about 60 cards including second cards on fixed turns.
  - §1 line 21 says junior high is the densest stage; this contradicts §6.
  - §1 line 22 says the game always ends by sending the child to a university; this contradicts §6.0.
  - The `_readme` in `calendar.json` still describes 92 turns.


- **Age formula**: `ageMonths = 72 + (g−1)·12 + ((month − 9 + 12) % 12)`. For the old 6 segments this gives exactly the old `k*2`, so A1 can be behavior-neutral.
- **`atMonths`** resolves to the first turn whose `ageMonths` is at or above the value. On the old calendar 32 resolves to turn 10, and `{stage:'國小低', i:0}` resolves to turn 20, the same turns as today.
- **Turn index ranges under 72**:

| Stage | Turns |
|---|---|
| 嬰幼兒 | 0–7 |
| 幼兒園 | 8–23 |
| 國小低 | 24–33 |
| 國小中高 | 34–49 |
| 國中 | 50–62 |
| 高中 | 63–68 |
| 離家 | 69–71 |

- Start-from-grade-1 begins at turn **24** and plays 48 turns.
- `buildTimeline` also stamps `tl.newAge = true` on the first turn of each new `floor(ageMonths/12)`. The yearly card (B3) uses this, so turn.js isn't edited again.
- **Fixed-event density in 國小中高** (4 turns a year): if every event ran every year, 3 of 4 turns would be fixed. Proposal:
  - `fx_sports_day` every year in 期中到寒假 (rule 17).
  - `fx_teacher_meet` only in grades 3 and 5, in 開學之後.
  - `fx_field_trip` only in grades 4 and 6, in 下學期.
  - `fx_graduation` in grade 6, 期末與暑假.
  - Result: 2 of 4 turns are fixed, the same 50% as before.
  - 國小低 keeps all of them, so 3 of 5 turns are fixed; measure this against the `nonfixed-share` target.

### 2.2 Habits auto-apply from junior high (B2; depends on A3 and A4)

**`data/habits.json`** has 5 families:

| Family | Patterns (tags detected) | Applied tag |
|---|---|---|
| 回家第一句 (§4.34) | 問功課分數 {只問成績或學校怎樣} / 問好玩的 {問具體的事, 觀察與參與} / 先抱一下 {放下手機陪, 先接住情緒} / 累到只說回來了 {敷衍帶過} | same, first tag |

| 考後 (§4.1) | 一起看卡在哪 {一起看哪裡卡住, 先接住情緒} / 罵或比較 {罵一頓, 拿別人比較, 只看成績} / 補了就好 {送去補習, 說沒關係就結束} | |
| 餐桌 (§4.33) | 放下手機 {放下手機陪} / 各滑各的 {一邊滑手機一邊回} | |

- **Each family carries** `lookback {國小中高:1, 國小低:0.6, 幼兒園:0.3}`, `minWeight 2.5`, `dominance 0.5`, `scale {國中:0.5, 高中:0.4, 離家:0}`, `shiftAt {國中:3, 高中:3}`, `line`/`lineIf` per pattern, and a `mixed` pattern with a neutral line and no tags.
- **Detection** (`js/engine/habits.js detectHabits(st, data)`, run once at the first 國中 turn and once at the start-from-grade-1 equivalent):
  - Look only at `hist` entries whose card has `habit:<family>`.
  - Weight each matching tag by `lookback[stage]`.
  - The winner needs at least `minWeight` total and at least `dominance` share; otherwise the family is `mixed`.
  - Requires annotating existing elementary cards with `habit` (e.g. `hi_exam`, the exam-paper cards → 考後; phone follow-ups → 手機規則; homework cards → 讀書節奏). C0 writes 2 new first-words cards (`lo_home_first`, `hi_home_first`) with the six §4.34 options.
- **Auto-apply** (`autoHabit(st, data, tl)`, the one hook in `turn.js startTurn`):
  - On each 國中/高中 turn, pick one non-mixed family round-robin (rngAt tie-break).
  - Call `applyTags(st, applyTags, T, {scale, approach:false, initiate: pattern.initiate})`.
  - Push `{turn, family, pattern, line}` into `st.autoLog`, **not** `hist`, so `keyMoments` and simulate's card lookups are unaffected.
  - No visible effects; the dose meter is not touched.
- **One-line display**: return `habitLine` in the `startTurn` result. The UI renders it like a load line, gray, above the card or quiet line.
  - `lineIf` switches on gates (A4 adds gates for 親子信任 high/low), so the child's reply hardens with history.
  - Examples: "你進門說『功課寫完了沒？』，房間裡傳來一聲『寫完了』。" and "你一進門，他從房間喊了一聲『你回來了』。"
- **Changing a habit**:
  - In junior and senior high, the `習慣_回家` card (pool weight 1.5) and the 主動找他 cards carry `choice.habitPattern`.
  - Choosing a pattern different from the current one increments `st.habits[f].shift.n`; choosing the old pattern resets it; at `shiftAt` the pattern flips.
  - `ctx.習慣改變_<f>` = n lets `resultIf` show the cold first replies (rule 22: the child's reaction may change, the parent's act may not).
- **Approach exit fix**: a non-mixed pattern flagged `initiate:true` passes `initiate` through to `updateApproach`, so a changed habit keeps earning exit credit. That makes `高中:6` reachable over about 2 years.
- **Lint** (`tools/lint/habits.mjs`): every `apply` tag exists; every family has ≥1 annotated card in 國小低 and in 國小中高; line pairs follow rule 12.
- **Tests** (`tests/habits.test.mjs`): detection determinism, mixed fallback, scale applied, shift after 3, autoLog only, and no hidden writes outside `tags.js` (existing Proxy guard).

### 2.3 Yearly summary card (B3)

**`data/yearly.json`**:
```json
{"togetherLine":"今年和你一起陪 {age} 歲孩子的父母有 {n} 位。",
 "eventsPerYear":3,
 "ages":{"1":{"facts":[{"id":"y1_words","text":"…","kind":"調查|里程碑","evidence":"強|中|初步|現場經驗|里程碑","if":"城鄉 == '偏鄉'",
```

- **`js/engine/yearly.js`**: pure `yearSummary(st, data, turn, snap)`.
  - Returns `{age, events, fact, together}` when `timeline[turn].newAge`.
  - `events`: up to 3 `hist` entries from the past 12 months, ranked by |estimateDeltas|, showing only title and label, never scores.
  - `fact`: chosen by `rngAt(seed, age, 'yearly')` among facts whose `if` passes.
  - `together`: `snap?.byAge?.[age]` or null.
- **UI** (`js/main.js`, `js/ui.js`):
  - the card shows before `beginTurn` on newAge turns;
  - buttons "繼續" and "以後都跳過", stored in `localStorage gwy.prefs.skipYearly`, outside the save;
  - in auto mode it shows for 3 s and then continues.
- **Tests** (`tests/yearly.test.mjs`): `JSON.stringify(st)` is identical before and after; ≤3 events; an empty year is handled.
- **Lint** (`tools/lint/yearly.mjs`):
  - every age 1–18 has ≥1 fact;
  - `evidence` is in the enum;
  - every fact has `source.url`, or is a milestone with `source.who`;
  - a fact containing a digit must not be marked 【未證實】/【憑記憶】 in `docs/research/yearly.md`;
  - text is ≤ 80 characters;
  - comparison words such as 比其他 or 排名 are forbidden.

## 6. Risks and what to measure with thousand-game runs

Each run: 1000 games per bot via `tools/simulate.mjs`, plus the human-mix runs in check-design.

1. **Density shift (A2)**:
   - avoid-enter rate by stage of entry;
   - 3C-window and streak-record firing rates;
   - `nonfixed-share`; `followup-delay` in months;
   - route/combo shares against A0 (≤ 5 percentage points).
2. **Turn-based windows**: compare `routePosition` over the last 12 points vs the last 36 months. The share of 未成形 must not jump by more than 10 percentage points.
3. **"No way back" vs "early doesn't matter"**: R² of final 親子 axis on state at turn 50 must be in 0.5–0.8. Plus the four plasticity bot gaps (§2.1).
4. **Double counting** (auto habits + plasticity + anchor): auto-applied |Δ| must be ≤ 35% of total |Δ| over turns 50–71; per-edge `maxGame` usage ≤ 100%.
5. **Approach in the teen stages**: exit rate by stage among games in the state ≥ 20% for 國中 and ≥ 10% for 高中, for `lateStarter`.
6. **Paths**:
   - school-type and destination shares, by `econ` and by bot;
   - dropout 2–10%;
   - wellbeing gap between destinations ≤ 6.
7. **Sensitive-event rates** (design estimates; ask the reviewers): gender-identity disclosure 3–8% of games; DV witnessing ≤ 3%, and only with a co-resident partner plus conflict history.
8. **Text and obviousness**:
   - surface, shortest and warmest bots within ±8 on new pools;
   - `obviousness --context` on teen cards: Q1 ≤ 60%, Q2 ≤ 50%.
9. **Performance**: `npm run bench` ≤ 20 s per 200 games per strategy; CI check-design 3000 must finish well inside the 20-minute job timeout. Measure in A2 and E1.
10. **Golden churn**: every package that changes signatures writes CHANGES.md reasons; the 1 start-from-grade-1 golden family verifies the start at turn 24.
11. **Yearly card**: the deep-equal test; every age has a sourced fact; the together line is hidden when the snapshot is null.
12. **Stats**: KV writes per day on the shared account are ≤ 1000; D1 rows read per snapshot stay bounded by the 7-day window.
